First, the basics

What is probability?

Probability is how likely something is to happen, from 0 (never) to 1 (always). We usually say it as a percent.

Probability = ways it can happen ÷ all possible outcomes

Why it matters here: customer demand is random, like a dice roll. If you usually sell 10 drinks and one week you sell 16, that's a lucky roll, not a new normal. A single rare outcome tells you very little. Over many weeks, results settle around the average. Managers who forget this overreact to one week and crack the whip.
